# Travel and expense policy (SAMPLE)

## Claiming expenses
- Submit claims in the expense system within 30 days, with a receipt or tax invoice for every item over ฿200.
- Claims are paid with the next salary when approved before the 20th of the month.

## Limits
- Meals while travelling upcountry: up to ฿400 per day.
- Hotels: up to ฿1,800 per night upcountry, ฿2,500 per night in Bangkok (for staff based elsewhere).
- Taxi or ride-hailing: allowed for client visits and after 21:00. Keep the receipt.
- Using your own car: ฿5 per km, with the route recorded in the claim.

## Approval
- Up to ฿5,000: your manager.
- ฿5,001–฿30,000: department head.
- Over ฿30,000 or any overseas travel: managing director, approved before booking.

## Not reimbursed
Alcohol (unless entertaining clients with prior approval), traffic fines, personal entertainment, and upgrades to business class.
