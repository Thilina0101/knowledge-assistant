# Leave policy / นโยบายการลา (SAMPLE)

## Annual leave / ลาพักร้อน
- Employees who have worked at least 1 year get 10 days of paid annual leave per year.
- From the 3rd year of service: 12 days. From the 5th year: 15 days.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. They must be used by 31 March.
- Request annual leave at least 3 working days in advance in the HR system, and get your manager's approval.

พนักงานที่ทำงานครบ 1 ปี มีสิทธิ์ลาพักร้อน 10 วันต่อปี และสะสมวันที่ไม่ได้ใช้ไปปีถัดไปได้ไม่เกิน 5 วัน

## Sick leave / ลาป่วย
- Up to 30 days of paid sick leave per year, as required by Thai labour law.
- Sick leave of 3 days or more in a row needs a medical certificate.
- Tell your manager before 9:00 on the day you are sick.

## Personal leave / ลากิจ
- 3 days of paid personal leave per year, for necessary personal business (e.g. government paperwork, family events).

## Other leave
- Maternity leave: 98 days, of which 45 days are paid by the company.
- Ordination leave (บวช): up to 15 days paid, once during employment, after 1 year of service.
