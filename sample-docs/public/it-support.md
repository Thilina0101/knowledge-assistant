# IT support guide (SAMPLE)

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are changed every 180 days.
To reset a forgotten password, go to https://password.northwind.example from any device, or ask the IT helpdesk (ext. 300).
Never send your password by email or chat. IT staff will never ask for it.

## Wi-Fi
Staff Wi-Fi: "Northwind-Staff" (log in with your company account).
Guest Wi-Fi: "Northwind-Guest". The password is on the reception desk and changes every Monday.

## Laptops
New staff receive a laptop on their first day. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within 24 hours.
Personal software may only be installed after IT approval.

## Security incidents
If you click a suspicious link or see unusual activity, disconnect from the network and call ext. 300 immediately.
